## The entry

> My smart fridge makes me watch a 30-second ad before it gives me water, so now I pay a monthly subscription to stay hydrated in my own kitchen.

One line, 143 characters. The roast aims at the smart fridge itself — its
screen and its water dispenser — not at kitchens in general. The setup is
an everyday smart-fridge annoyance (an ad-gated dispenser) and the punchline
turns it: hydration in your own kitchen now comes with a monthly
subscription. It dodges the obvious cracks about the fridge ordering
groceries or watching you eat, and it says nothing that explains its own
joke.

## What this repo does
